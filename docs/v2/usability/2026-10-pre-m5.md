## Three-person test (about 10 minutes each, no instructions)

Open the site, hand over the device, and say only: "This is a robot website. Explore it, and think out loud." Don't help. Note what they do.

### Person 1 (background: )

- What did they do in the first 30 seconds?
- Where did they get stuck or confused?
- Did they manage to drive the robot? (yes / no / with help)
- Did they understand what the coloured areas and lines on the floor were?
