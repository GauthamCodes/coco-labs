# COCO Lab v2.0: the Glass-box Arena (DRAFT)

> **Draft, not published.** Written by the agent at M3.8 and updated at M4.10
> for the 3D Arena (decision D5: v2.0 launches with M4); releases, tags and
> their texts are Gautham's (README §9). Every number below cites the
> `docs/RESULTS.md` section it is copied from; nothing here is measured for
> the first time. The rows Gautham measures (the phone rows, the five-person
> usability test) are left blank until he does.

COCO Lab teaches how a mobile robot plans, localises, maps, moves and
decides, by showing the computation itself. v2 turns the five v1 labs into
one app in the browser, **in 3D**: you open the site and you are in COCO's
arena, behind the robot. Drive it with the keys or a joystick, hand it to
autonomy, watch the planner's search and the particle filter spread over the
floor, drop to top-down to inspect, race the planner's ghost, and open a
recorded run of the full stack in the same world. Four modes:

- **Learn:** seven missions. Every claim carries a learner label (MEASURED,
  TESTED, SIMULATION RESULT, SIMPLIFIED MODEL, ASSUMPTION, INFERENCE,
  UNRESOLVED) and evidence that must resolve, or the build fails.
- **Play:** six challenges scored by robotics metrics, with stars set by
  reference algorithms, and links anyone can re-score. New in 3D: **Race the
  planner's ghost**, the model following A*'s path; beat its time.
- **Sandbox:** the Arena. A model of COCO, its world and its algorithms,
  running in your browser in Python (Pyodide): the whole loop of planning,
  localisation, mapping, local control and the fetch mission, now up the
  ramps and onto the platforms, with a gated grasp. It is **kinematic**: no
  physics engine, no forces, slip or tipping, and a "MODEL · kinematic"
  badge says so whenever it drives.
- **Case Files:** every recorded run of the full ROS 2 stack in Gazebo,
  failures included, beside the model on the same scenario.

**No physical robot exists.** Every badge says what you are looking at:
MODEL (the browser) or STACK (the full ROS 2 stack in Gazebo, recorded).

## The 3D Arena, measured

*(RESULTS: "COCO Lab v2 · M4: the 3D Arena against its acceptance criteria",
unless noted)*

- 60 fps on a laptop's RTX 4050 at the high tier with the fetch mission's
  layers (median 61, slowest second 60, in every run), and on its integrated
  Intel graphics at the medium tier; key press to visible motion, median
  45.1 ms (follow camera, 100 presses).
- On emulated 4G (5 cold runs): the first computation on screen by 3,694 ms,
  the 3D world with the robot by 3,605 ms, the live model by 8,521 ms.
- The whole first scene is 567,792 B on a desktop and 171,532 B on a phone
  (meshopt-compressed).
- Every Case File opens in 3D within 2 s on emulated 4G, 100 of 100 (the
  slowest 1,773 ms; the robot's own mesh follows its placeholder body).
- 30 minutes of driving, cameras and lenses: no lost WebGL context, no
  console error, no long task.
- The model's terrain against Gazebo's ground truth on Lab 4's 17 fetches:
  height within 7.4 mm (p95) on the slopes, pitch within 1.37°. *(RESULTS:
  "COCO Lab v2 · M4.3")*
- The terrain model gives the same result, tick for tick, in Chromium,
  Firefox, WebKit and Python in Node: 100 sessions, 70,000 ticks each.
  *(RESULTS: "COCO Lab v2 · M4.3")*

## What is measured

**The Arena model is deterministic across engines.**
- 100 sessions × 150 ticks, with localisation noise, mapping and the fetch
  mission, in Chromium, Firefox, WebKit and Pyodide-in-Node: 0 ticks differ.
  *(RESULTS: "COCO Lab v2 · M2 — the whole loop against its acceptance
  criteria")*

**It is fast on a laptop** (balanced, on AC):
- 58–62 fps in every second of 200 s on the fetch mission with every lens's
  layers *(RESULTS: "COCO Lab v2 · M2 independent review: re-measured after
  the reboot")*
- goal after a planner change: median 40.3 ms, max 66.8 ms *(same)*
- first visible computation on emulated 4G: median 3,201 ms *(same)*

**Case Files.**
- 100 recordings of the full stack, every cited checksum matched (73 of 73)
  *(RESULTS: "COCO Lab v2 · M3.3: the Case Files")*; now with the stack's
  recorded height, roll and pitch, 40,019,390 B *(RESULTS: "COCO Lab v2 ·
  M4")*
- in the browser: first frame at most 279 ms, seek at most 45 ms *(RESULTS:
  "COCO Lab v2 · M3.3")*

**Play, re-scored anywhere.**
- 100 generated submissions, scored by the page in Chromium
- `coco verify` (Node, no browser) gave 100 of 100 identical result hashes
- every link reproduced exactly in Chromium, Firefox and WebKit, 100 of 100
  each

*(RESULTS: "COCO Lab v2 · M3.6: `coco verify`, and links across three
browsers")*

**The model and the stack, side by side** (MODEL against STACK).
- The Arena's LiDAR casts beam for beam as Lab 2's Sketch on the Nav2 map,
  so its gap against Gazebo is Lab 2's: median 2.2 mm, p95 9.3 cm. *(RESULTS:
  "COCO Lab v2 · M2 — the whole loop…", fidelity bullet)*
- Told it was 3.4 m from where it stood, every Nav2 controller aborted in
  its first cycle, 9 of 9 on the stack, and the model's did too, 15 of 15.
  That is run 15's mechanism, not its every detail. *(RESULTS: "The
  controller matrix"; docs/v2/M2_MOVE_COMPARISON.md)*
