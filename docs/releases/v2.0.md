COCO Lab is a browser app for driving a robot, comparing its decisions and opening
recorded ROS runs. It opens on COCO idling under a warehouse work light, with three
clear doors:

- **Play:** drive in Free roam, take on the existing challenges or race the planner's
  ghost. A full-screen HUD keeps the objective, robot map, speed and actions in view.
  Robot vision reveals the scan area, search, costmap and active localization data.
  Scores show the actual metrics and public star thresholds; replay and share a run.
- **Compare:** put existing Python reference algorithms on the same map, start,
  goal and seed. Inspect layered costmaps, compare expanded cells and paths, scrub
  a shared timeline, and follow tours whose claims link to committed evidence.
  Global planning, local control and localization keep their differences visible,
  including failures and unresolved results.
- **Research:** search the Recordings library by level, algorithm or controller,
  outcome and evidence class. Open a Case File or converted recording in the viewer,
  or inspect the original explanations and evidence. Failed runs stay in the library.

The Night Shift design includes dark and light themes, self-hosted fonts, responsive
phone controls, reduced motion and shared Settings. Returning visitors can use a
static asset cache. The aggregate counter is off until Gautham configures it.
Existing links and the frozen `/v1/` archive keep their homes. Live Stack (simulated)
is linked from About, alongside credits and licences.

**MODEL means a kinematic Python model. STACK means recorded ROS in Gazebo. No
physical robot exists.** The browser does not simulate forces, wheel slip, tipping
or contact physics. COCO's gripper has fingers and a magnet; the magnet holds the
object. Display polish does not change that model or the recorded evidence.

The Depot, new games, Grasp Assist, experiments, a visitor ROS bridge and cloud
sessions remain future work. This launch focuses the product that already exists.
